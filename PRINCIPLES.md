# Product principles

PracticeGraph exists to make people **more capable over time**, not more
engaged. These commitments bind every feature; several are enforced by
automated tests, and a feature that cannot honor them does not ship.

## 1. Measure behavior, never optimize engagement

Success is user capability and wellbeing over time — explicitly **not**
usage. No metric of ours frames more prompts, more tokens, or more time in
an AI tool as achievement. Usage numbers appear as neutral accounting
(cost, tokens, turns), never as goals.

## 2. No dark patterns — by name

- **No streaks.** Unbroken-daily-usage counters turn rest days into losses
  and volume into virtue. The report shows *active days* as a neutral fact
  and never counts consecutive-day chains. (Long *unbroken working
  stretches* are surfaced the other way around — as a pause reminder.)
- **No badges, levels, or leaderboards.** Default wellbeing-facing observations
  show their evidence, period, and confidence. Numeric heuristics are explicitly
  experimental diagnostics, kept secondary and never comparative.
- **No loss-aversion or manufactured urgency.** Nudges are calm,
  dismissible, capped (at most two observation tips a day, one break nudge),
  and silent during quiet hours.
- **No variable-reward notifications.** Alerts are few, predictable, and
  informational; the notification channel must never become its own
  slot machine.

## 3. A qualified observation leads

The default Today surface leads with one evidence-gated work-pattern observation,
including its period, confidence, and caveat. Coaching remains positive-first and
observational — "if the pattern is not how you want to work" — never a judgment,
diagnosis, or causal claim.

## 4. Behavioral language only

Product copy describes observable behavior (blocks, pauses, switches,
follow-up timing). A forbidden-lexicon test bans clinical and
neuro-mechanistic vocabulary from every surface — including catalog content
a server tries to push. We adopt what the attention research supports and
drop its marketing language.

## 5. Autonomy is a feature

Every tip is permanently dismissible, every suggestion supports
never-show-again, focus coaching has a master switch, and consent for
sharing anything is off by default. Nudges inform and offer; they never
coerce, and turning them off is never punished.

## 6. Judgment stays in the loop

The product's coaching pushes one direction: keep the human's own judgment
engaged — read before the next ask, review where mistakes cost most,
explain a change back to yourself, solve the small ones unaided. The tool
is a sidekick that makes the engineer sharper, never a substitute that
makes the engineer optional.

## 7. Local first, forever

Wellbeing and performance data never leave the machine (enforced by wire
field-name scans). What can be shared — with explicit opt-in — is closed,
anonymous, k-anonymity-suppressed aggregates, previewable byte-for-byte
before anything is sent.
