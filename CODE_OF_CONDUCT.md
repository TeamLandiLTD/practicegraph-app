# Code of conduct

PracticeGraph is built by people who want to work with AI more clearly, and the project's spaces should feel the way the product does: observational, specific and never judgmental of the person.

## Our standards

Everyone who participates in this project's issues, pull requests, discussions and releases agrees to:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, evidence and code, not with people.
- Be specific. Describe what you observed, what you expected and how you checked it.
- Be careful with other people's data. Never post logs, prompts, tokens, private paths or an unredacted database, yours or anyone else's, and never ask others to.
- Assume good faith and give the benefit of the doubt to newcomers.
- Accept review. Maintainers may decline a change that conflicts with the [product principles](PRINCIPLES.md); that is a decision about the product, not about you.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately to the maintainers at hello@teamlandi.com. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove content, and warn, temporarily restrict or permanently remove anyone whose behavior violates this code. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce it in good faith may face the same consequences.

This document draws on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
