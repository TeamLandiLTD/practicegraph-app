PracticeGraph is installed

It is opening now. Look for its icon in the menu bar: click it for the dashboard, or right-click for settings such as Open at Login.

The first time, it reads your existing history, which can take a few minutes on a long one. The pages fill in as it goes.

To remove background refresh later, run launchctl bootout gui/$(id -u)/dev.practicegraph.agent and delete ~/Library/LaunchAgents/dev.practicegraph.agent.plist.
