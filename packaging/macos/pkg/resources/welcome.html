Welcome to PracticeGraph

PracticeGraph reads the logs your AI coding tools already keep on this Mac and shows how you use them: cost at API prices, models, tools and your work rhythm.

  • Everything is analysed on this Mac. Your logs and readings never leave it.
  • Upgrading? The installer closes a running PracticeGraph first. Your history and settings are kept.
  • Background refresh keeps model guidance and prices current while the app is closed. You can untick it under Customize.

PracticeGraph is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0.
